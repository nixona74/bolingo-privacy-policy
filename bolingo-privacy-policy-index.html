Bolingo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Bolingo is a dating and social discovery app. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Bolingo, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you. By creating an account or using Bolingo, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide: We may collect information you enter or upload, including your name or display name, email address, phone number, date of birth or age, gender, relationship preferences, biography, occupation, education, profile photographs, and other profile details. Some profile details may reveal sensitive or personal aspects of your life. Only provide information you are comfortable sharing.

Messages and interactions: We process messages, match and favourite activity, conversation-related information, and other content or actions you choose to send through the app. Messages are made available to the conversation participants.

Location and venue information: We may process the city and country on your profile and, when you grant permission and use a location-based feature, device location information such as coordinates. Location is used to support relevant discovery and nearby features. You can manage device location permission in your device settings; some features may not work without it. Bolingo is not intended to continuously track your movements.

Safety and verification information: If you use profile verification or safety features, we may process the information and evidence you submit, reports you make, and related review records. Safe Date Check-In may include a date plan, venue, planned time, notes, and a trusted contact's name and phone number. Provide another person's contact details only when you are authorised to do so.

Technical information: The app and its service providers may process information needed to operate and protect the service, such as device and app information, log data, diagnostic information, and notification tokens where notifications are enabled.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; display and manage profiles; provide discovery, matching, favourites and messaging; support hotels and restaurants features; provide verification, reporting and safety tools; send service-related notifications; respond to support requests; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; maintain, troubleshoot and improve Bolingo; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Profile visibility and sharing

Information you add to your dating profile, including profile photographs and profile details, may be shown to other Bolingo users according to the app's features and settings. Messages and shared content are available to the people in the relevant conversation. Do not include private information in a public-facing profile unless you intend other users to see it.

We may share information with service providers that help operate Bolingo, such as our database, authentication and file-storage provider, Supabase, and infrastructure or notification providers used by the app. These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us and under their applicable terms and safeguards.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users or the public, to investigate suspected fraud or abuse, or to protect the rights, safety and security of Bolingo and its users. If Bolingo is involved in a business transfer, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

4. Advertising and sale of information

Bolingo does not currently display advertisements. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. If advertising or other materially different data uses are introduced, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by law.

5. Data storage, security and international transfers

Bolingo uses Supabase for certain backend services, which may include account authentication, database and file storage. Information may be stored or processed in countries other than the country where you live, depending on the location of our service providers and their infrastructure. Those countries may have different data-protection laws.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No online service or method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Bolingo, maintain account and service records, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, protect users, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it is needed. Some information may remain in backups or records for a limited period after deletion.

To request deletion of your Bolingo account and associated personal information, email nixona2024@gmail.com from your registered email address and include the phone number or account details needed to identify your account. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting. We will process requests in accordance with applicable law; some information may need to be retained where legally permitted or required.

7. Your choices and rights

You can review and update certain profile information in the app. You can manage device permissions, including location and notifications, through your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on certain personal information, or to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact us using the email below.

8. Children's privacy and age requirement

Bolingo is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to create or maintain an account. If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information to Bolingo, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Third-party services and links

Bolingo may contain links to third-party websites or services, including hotels or restaurants. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties; review their policies before providing information to them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Bolingo changes or when required by law. We will revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app or by other reasonable means. Your continued use of Bolingo after an updated policy takes effect is subject to the updated policy.
