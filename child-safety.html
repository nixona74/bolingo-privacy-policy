# Bolingo – Child Safety Standards **Effective date:** November 5, 2026 ## 1. Our Commitment Bolingo is a dating and social connection platform intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and maintaining a safe environment for our users. ## 2. Zero Tolerance for Child Exploitation Bolingo strictly prohibits: - Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM). - Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors. - Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes. - Sharing, requesting, creating, or distributing content that sexually exploits children. - Any activity that facilitates child trafficking or exploitation. ## 3. Age Restrictions Bolingo is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Individuals under 18 are not permitted to create or use an account. Accounts found to belong to minors may be suspended or removed. ## 4. Reporting and Enforcement Users can report inappropriate profiles, messages, or suspicious activity through available reporting features in Bolingo. Reports involving suspected child exploitation are treated as a priority. Bolingo will investigate reports, remove prohibited content, and suspend or terminate accounts involved in violations, as appropriate. ## 5. Cooperation with Authorities Bolingo will cooperate with relevant law enforcement and child protection authorities and report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the appropriate regional or national authority where required by applicable law. ## 6. Child Safety Contact For child safety concerns, CSAE reports, or questions about these standards, contact: **Bolingo Child Safety Team** Email: nixona2024@gmail.com This contact is responsible for receiving child safety concerns and responding to Google Play inquiries about Bolingo's child safety practices. ## 7. Policy Updates Bolingo may update these standards as necessary to improve child protection and comply with applicable laws and Google Play requirements. **Bolingo – Child Safety Standards**
